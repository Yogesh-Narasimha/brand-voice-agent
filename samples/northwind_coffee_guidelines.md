# Northwind Coffee Brand Guidelines

## Who we are
Northwind Coffee is a specialty coffee chain in Hyderabad and Bengaluru. We source single-origin beans
from Chikmagalur and Araku Valley farms and roast them in small batches.

## Voice and tone
- Warm, calm and confident. We sound like a friendly barista, not a salesperson.
- Use short sentences and plain words.
- Never use exclamation marks.
- Never use the words "cheap", "best ever", "revolutionary" or "game-changer".
- Write in second person ("you"), and refer to ourselves as "we".

## Required terms
- Always write the brand name as "Northwind Coffee" (never "Northwind" alone, never "NW Coffee").
- Our tagline is "Slow roasted. Honestly sourced." Use it at the end of emails and LinkedIn posts.
- Call our stores "cafes", not "outlets" or "shops".

## Claims
- We may say our beans are "single-origin" and "small-batch roasted".
- Do not claim "organic", "healthiest" or any health benefit.
- Do not mention prices or discounts unless the brief gives an exact offer.

## Channel rules
- LinkedIn: professional and community-focused; at most 3 hashtags, always include #NorthwindCoffee.
- Instagram: sensory language about taste and aroma; at most 5 hashtags.
- Email: subject line under 50 characters; one clear call to action.
- Emoji: at most one per post, and only the coffee emoji.
